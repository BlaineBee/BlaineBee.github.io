## Who I am

I've had a passion for technology since I was four years old, and that curiosity has grown into a commitment to building a career in information technology and cybersecurity. I've used Windows since the release of Windows Vista, and over the years I've developed a strong understanding of system navigation, troubleshooting, configuration, and everyday administration.

My goal is to grow into a role where I can help organizations solve technical problems, improve security, and build reliable systems.

## Background

My background includes customer support, technical troubleshooting, operations, and working in technology-driven environments. Through roles supporting customers, managing sensitive information, and resolving complex issues, I've developed strong communication skills, attention to detail, and the ability to troubleshoot problems efficiently.

My experience in customer relations, financial services, retail technology, and operations has strengthened my ability to communicate clearly, solve problems under pressure, and support users with patience and professionalism. I approach IT challenges from both a systems perspective and a user perspective.
